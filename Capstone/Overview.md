# Capstone Overview

The capstone is your opportunity to showcase what you have learned during the bootcamp. It is not an exam to pass or fail.

## Process

On Monday, August 3, you can review the team assignments and rubric and provide any feedback on those items.

As well, you can review the initial high level specification that outlines the scope of the project. The initial spec will describe the overall context and structure of the project. 

During the week of Aug 3, you will also receive detailed specifications for the individual components identified in the high level specification.

August 11-13 are reserved for your team to work on your capstone project during the scheduled class time.

On August 14, every team will present their project to the invited guests which will include some of your managers and BoA SMEs.


## Team Orientation

There will be an overall assessment of your team's capstone project. It is important to remember that this is not an individual assessment, and no one will be singled out in the feedback. 

One of the criteria for assessement will be how well you have worked together as a team. One of the observable measures we will be relying on is the equal participation of all tema members in the presentation.

## Preliminaries

Since you will be working on the same codebase as a team, you should develop a way to share and integrate your work as you develop your project.

Since you will be doing your work in your class VMs, the following is a suggested process that worked well for the last cohort

1. Each team member should create a class GitHub account. If you want to use an existing GitHub account, you are free to do so, but it is probably safer from a security perspective to use a throwaway GitHub account for the capstone.
2. There should be a project GitHub account where the class work is integrated. One of the team members can create it and add the other team members as collaborators.
3. Modularize your project so that team members can work on individual components without unintended side effects.
